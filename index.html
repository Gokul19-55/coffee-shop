About Us

At Rajinimurugan Coffee Shop, we believe life is too short for bad coffee — and even shorter without a good vada on the side! Born in the streets of Madurai with a filter kaapi in one hand and a big smile in the other, we serve strong coffee that can wake the dead (or at least your Monday morning brain). Our snacks are crunchy, our dosas are crispy, and our staff smiles wider than a Tamil movie hero's intro scene. Whether you're a kaapi addict, a tea enthusiast, or just someone who wandered in for the AC, we promise good vibes, great food, and coffee.
